Founded by industrial rope access technicians and building envelope engineers, Elevex provides high-consequence exterior cleaning for premier commercial skyscrapers, hotel towers, and strata properties.

Our Pedigree & History

Born on the Ropes, Built for Commercial Towers

Elevex originated over 12 years ago when our founding directors recognized a critical gap in the commercial building maintenance industry: general cleaning companies lacked the engineering rigor required for extreme heights, while industrial offshore rope companies lacked customer-centric property management service standards.

We brought offshore industrial rope access safety rigor to the urban skyline. Today, Elevex maintains over 450 commercial towers annually, delivering spotless architectural glass maintenance, zero-incident safety records, and friction-free coordination for property managers.
