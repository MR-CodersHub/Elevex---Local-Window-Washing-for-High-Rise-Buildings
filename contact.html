Survey Logistics

What to Expect During a Site Assessment

Everything property managers need to know prior to an on-roof technical inspection.

What do our engineers inspect during the roof survey?

We verify certified anchor eyebolts, davit arm sockets, parapet wall construction, edge protection requirements, roof plant layout, and ground exclusion zone feasibility. We also assess facade glass type and existing soil levels.

How long does it take to receive the formal quotation and RAMS?

Following the on-site survey, you will receive our complete fixed-price proposal within 24 to 48 hours, including drop diagrams, method outlines, and insurance certificates ready for strata committee or board review.

Is there any charge for the initial site assessment?

No. Initial architectural surveys and drop feasibility evaluations within our metropolitan service territory are provided 100% complimentary to building managers and property owners.
