Institutional Governance

The 5 Pillars of Our Zero-Harm Working-at-Height System

Every single job is governed by comprehensive Risk Assessments and Method Statements (RAMS) designed to insulate building owners and property managers from statutory liability.

1. Dual Independent Rope Rigging: Continuous two-point attachment with certified 15 kN static anchor redundancy.
2. Daily Pre-Drop Equipment Logging: Ropes, descenders, backup devices, and harnesses inspected before every descent.
3. Total Tool Tethering (Zero Drop Risk): Every squeegee, scraper, water applicator, and bucket secured with rated lanyards.
4. Ground Exclusion Zone Marshalling: Barricades, high-visibility warning signage, and dedicated spotters at street level.
5. On-Site Aerial Rescue Preparedness: Dedicated rescue haul kits and certified Level 3 supervisors on standby during every drop.
