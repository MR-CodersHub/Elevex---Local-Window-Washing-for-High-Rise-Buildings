Non-Negotiable Safety Protocols

Zero Compromise on High-Elevation Safety

High-rise facade maintenance is inherently high-consequence work. Our operational framework incorporates military-grade procedural discipline, digital risk profiling, and redundant equipment architecture.

Dual Independent Rope Redundancy

Every technician is rigged to two completely independent 11mm low-stretch kernmantle ropes: a primary Working Line and an independent Backup Fall-Arrest Line. In the theoretical failure of any single component, the backup line engages instantly with zero impact shock.

Certified Anchor Load Verification: Every roof anchor point is physically examined and proof-tested prior to line connection.
100% Tool Tethering Protocol: Every squeegee, scraper, and bucket is tethered via shock-absorbing lanyards to prevent dropped objects.
Strict Wind Speed Stand-Down: Real-time anemometer telemetry triggers immediate operations pause if wind gusts exceed 25 mph (40 km/h).

Operational Workflow

The 6-Stage Safe Access Protocol

From the initial architectural assessment to the final quality sign-off, our systematic process ensures absolute safety and spotless results on every elevation.

Phase 01 01

Initial Scope & Blueprint Audit

We review architectural CAD blueprints, elevation heights, glass square footage, anchor availability, and cleaning history to determine viable access strategies.

Feasibility & Elevation Access Plan
Phase 02 02

On-Site Technical Roof Survey

A Level 3 Rigging Supervisor inspects roof parapets, static lines, eyebolts, davit arms, and ground exclusion boundaries with building engineering teams.

Anchor Load Testing & Drop Map
Phase 03 03

RAMS & Method Planning

Creation of site-specific Risk Assessments and Method Statements detailing rigging mechanics, drop order, weather limits, and emergency aerial rescue plans.

Comprehensive RAMS & Rescue Protocol
Phase 04 04

Safe Rigging & Exclusion Zones

Dual ropes rigged to certified points, roof edge protection installed, and ground hazard cones with spotters deployed prior to any technician descent.

100% Perimeter Exclusion & Dual Line Rig
Phase 05 05

Pure Water & Detailing Clean

Systematic top-to-bottom facade descents using multi-stage de-ionized pure water, architectural squeegees, and microfiber frame detailing for streak-free glass.

0-TDS Spotless Optical Finish
Phase 06 06

Inspection & Digital Sign-Off

Final ground and roof audits, equipment de-rigging, and prompt upload of before-and-after condition reports to the client's Building Manager Portal.
