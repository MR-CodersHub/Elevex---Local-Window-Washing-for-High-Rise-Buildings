Working-at-Height Safety & Rigging Governance

High-elevation building maintenance requires relentless discipline. Our Zero-Harm operational standard protects your tenants, building assets, the public below, and our technicians on the ropes.

Rigging Engineering

The Dual-Rope Redundancy Principle

At Elevex, no technician is ever suspended on a single line. Our standard operating procedure dictates that every operative is permanently linked to two independent lines anchored to separate certified points.

Working Line: 11mm static low-stretch kernmantle rope paired with certified descender devices allowing micro-metered descent and controlled positioning.
Safety Backup Line: An independent parallel line equipped with an automatic mobile fall-arrester that moves freely with the technician and locks instantaneously without user intervention.
Parapet Edge Protection: High-density canvas rollers and articulated metal edge protectors deployed on all stone, concrete, and metal roof parapets to eliminate rope chafe.

Operational Documentation

Site-Specific RAMS Packages

Prior to setting foot on your rooftop, our Level 3 Supervisor compiles a comprehensive Risk Assessment & Method Statement (RAMS). This legally binding document outlines every operational hazard and mitigative control.

Architectural Hazard Matrix: Details roof parapet conditions, air handler exhaust proximity, high-voltage lines, and building protrusions.
Anchor Layout Mapping: Blueprint diagram showing each tested eyebolt and static line to be utilized on each elevation drop.
Dedicated Rescue Plan: Specific physical steps and designated rigging anchors for mid-air extraction within minutes.
