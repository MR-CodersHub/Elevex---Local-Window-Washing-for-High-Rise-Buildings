Facility Manager FAQs

Common Questions About High-Rise Access

Key considerations for building owners, strata councils, and facilities management professionals.

What happens if our building doesn't have certified roof anchors?

Our Level 3 rigging engineers inspect your roof structure. If eyebolts are uncertified or absent, we can either coordinate statutory pull-testing and certification, utilize temporary structural deadweight anchor systems, or deploy ground-based MEWP boom lifts.

How do you protect street pedestrians and ground retail below?

We establish physical ground exclusion zones with stanchions, high-visibility hazard bunting, and dedicated ground spotters. Furthermore, our 100% tool tethering protocol guarantees that zero equipment can physically fall.

How are high winds and inclement weather managed?

We monitor rooftop telemetry with digital anemometers. Operations pause immediately if sustained wind gusts exceed 25 mph (40 km/h). Rescheduling is automated at no penalty to the building manager.

Request an on-site architectural survey or upload building drawings to receive our comprehensive RAMS and fixed-price quote within 24 hours.
